# 180-Day PKI Evolution Roadmap

## Phase 1 (0-30 days): Baseline and exposure map

- Asset inventory with owner mapping.
- Expiration and revocation baseline.
- Validation endpoint health baseline.

## Phase 2 (31-75 days): Architecture controls

- Domain segmentation decisions.
- Template and policy profile review.
- Revocation and renewal SLA definitions.

## Phase 3 (76-120 days): Operationalization

- Runbooks and incident playbooks.
- Automation for renewals and reporting.
- KPI dashboards for lifecycle and validation.

## Phase 4 (121-180 days): Hardening and future readiness

- Crypto baseline review and migration candidates.
- Policy exception cleanup.
- Quarterly governance board model.
